# Glossario italiano · Italian glossary

The Italian version of Story Builder uses these terms everywhere: interface,
knowledge base, Story Doctor, the worked example and the AI partner. Keep them
consistent when you add or change text.

## Style

- Address the writer with **tu**. Buttons use the imperative: *Aggiungi*,
  *Elimina*, *Esporta*, *Apri*.
- **Sentence case** for titles and labels: *Incidente scatenante*, not
  *Incidente Scatenante*.
- Typography: apostrophe **’** (*l’idea*, *dell’atto*), quotes **“ ”**,
  ellipsis **…**, dash **—**.
- Prefer gender-neutral wording when talking about the writer or the audience
  (*chi scrive*, *il pubblico*); the protagonist follows the story.
- Chart labels must stay short: shorten rather than wrap.
- The book stays *Story* (Robert McKee). Chapter references: **cap. 8**,
  **Parte 2**. Film titles: the Italian release title when it is well known
  (*Il padrino*, *Quarto potere*), otherwise the original title.
